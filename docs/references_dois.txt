[--] ESDU 91022
     "Thermophysical Properties of Nitrous Oxide", ESDU International, 1991
     Subscription engineering-data series. Saturated-N2O correlations:
     Tc = 309.57 K, Pc = 7.251 MPa, rho_c = 452 kg/m^3.
     Access note: requires an ESDU subscription. If the team has no access,
     the correlations are reproduced in several open hybrid-rocket theses;
     cite ESDU as the primary regardless.

[--] NACA TN 4197
     Martin, D. J. (1958), "Summary of Flutter Experiences as a Guide to the
     Preliminary Design of Lifting Surfaces on Missiles"
     NTRS accession: 19930085030
     https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19930085030
     Free full text. The author himself calls the criteria "crude" -- hence
     driving it with an effective GJ from laminate theory plus a separate
     divergence check.

[--] NACA Report 496
     Theodorsen, T. (1935), "General Theory of Aerodynamic Instability and
     the Mechanism of Flutter"
     Bending-torsion flutter theory underlying TN 4197.

[--] NACA Report 1048
     Allen, H. J. and Perkins, E. W. (1951), "A Study of Effects of Viscosity
     on Flow over Slender Inclined Bodies of Revolution"
     Body cross-flow drag; corrects Barrowman at non-trivial alpha.

     Characteristics of Slender Finned Vehicles", M.S. thesis,
     The Catholic University of America

     Free full text.

[--] NASA RP-1311
